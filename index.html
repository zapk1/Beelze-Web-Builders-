Privacy & POPIA Compliance

Beelze Web Builders — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · POPIA Compliant

Beelze Web Builders ("we", "us", "our") is a website development business based in Limpopo, South Africa. We build, sell and maintain professional websites for companies and individuals across South Africa. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order, request a quote, or contact us, we collect: your full name, business/company name, phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, the type of website you want, and any extra details you choose to share. When you upload a website to our market, we store the website name, description, price and the HTML code you submit.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to: build and deliver your website, contact you about your order and payment, provide monthly maintenance and support, and keep a record of the transaction for accounting and tax purposes. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with any third party for marketing.

3. Payments & Banking Details

Payments are made via EFT / bank transfer to our GoTyme business account. We collect your payment reference to match your deposit to your order. We never store your card numbers or banking login details — all card payments are handled directly by your bank.

4. Data Storage

Your order details are stored securely in a cloud database accessible only to the business owner (admin). Uploaded website HTML is stored so buyers can preview and purchase the finished website. We retain order records for as long as legally required for tax and accounting purposes.

5. Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the right to: access the personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion of your information (where legally allowed), and object to processing. To exercise any of these rights, email us at ceo@beelzechat.co.za.

6. Cookies & Website Tracking

This website does not use tracking cookies to profile visitors. Analytics, if added, will be aggregated and anonymous. Any website we build for you will have its own cookie policy that you control.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 without a parent or guardian's consent.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your information: encrypted database storage, restricted admin access protected by a password, and secure payment references. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we do our best.

9. Information Officer (POPIA)

Our Information Officer for POPIA complaints is the CEO. Contact: ceo@beelzechat.co.za · 068 779 0223. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the updated date shown at the top.

Confirmation: By placing an order, uploading a website, or contacting Beelze Web Builders, you confirm that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and you consent to Beelze Web Builders processing your personal information as described above, in compliance with POPIA.
